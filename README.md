## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution:
We propose creating a digital platform called Farm2Market that connects farmers directly with customers, shops, supermarkets, hotels and wholesale buyers.

Through this platform, farmers can easily sell their products without depending on too many middlemen. They can upload details of their products, set reasonable prices and receive orders directly from buyers. Customers can also compare prices and buy fresh agricultural products at affordable rates.

Our platform will use AI technology to predict product demand, suggest suitable prices and find the best delivery routes. It will also provide transport support and secure online payments.

The main aim of Farm2Market is to help farmers earn more money, reduce prices for consumers and make the process of buying and selling agricultural products easier and faster.

## Technical Approach
1. First, we will create a website or mobile app where farmers and buyers can register and log in.
2. Farmers can upload their products, photos, quantity and prices.
3. Buyers can view the available products, compare prices and place orders directly with farmers.
4. We will use AI to predict product demand and suggest fair prices to farmers.
5. We will provide transport support to deliver products from farms to customers.
6. GPS and maps will help find the shortest and best delivery routes.
7. Online payment facilities will make transactions easy and secure.
8. Finally, farmers can earn better profits, and customers can buy fresh products at lower prices.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. We can easily develop this platform using existing technologies like Python and web development tools.
2. Farmers and buyers can use the app or website to buy and sell products easily.
3. AI can help predict product demand and suggest fair prices to farmers.
4. Direct selling reduces middlemen, helping farmers earn more and customers save money.
5. The platform can reduce delivery costs and can be expanded to more villages and cities in the future.

## Impact and Benefits
1. More Income for Farmers: Farmers can sell products directly and earn better profits.
2. Lower Prices for Customers: Customers can buy fresh products at affordable prices.
3. Less Middlemen: Direct connection between farmers and buyers reduces extra costs.
4. Faster Delivery: Smart routes help deliver products quickly and reduce transport costs.
5. Less Food Waste: AI helps predict demand and avoid excess production and unsold products.

## Research and References
1. We studied how farmers sell their products and found that middlemen reduce farmers' profits.
2. We researched e-NAM to understand how farmers can sell products through an online marketplace.
3. We studied how AI can predict product demand and suggest fair prices to farmers.
4. We researched GPS and Google Maps to understand how to find better delivery routes and reduce transport costs.
5. We explored Digital India and NPCI websites to learn how digital technology and online payments can make buying and selling easier.
